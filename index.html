About us

The Dalhousie Graduate Physics Society (DGPS) represents graduate students in the Department of Physics & Atmospheric Science at Dalhousie University in Halifax. We organize social events, help new students settle in, and give grad students a voice in the department.

Every physics graduate student is a member. Come to an event, or get in touch if you have an idea for something we should do.

What we do

Socials

Trivia nights, games, and seasonal get-togethers to meet people outside the lab.

Talks

Informal talks where grad students share their research with each other.

Support

Help for new students, and a link between grad students and the department.
